# 06 - Solar System Simulator

## Introduction

The Solar System is a gravitationally bound system consisting of the Sun and the objects that orbit it.

The Sun contains approximately 99.8% of the total mass of the Solar System, making it the dominant gravitational body.

Planets remain in orbit because of the balance between their forward velocity and the Sun's gravitational attraction.

This chapter explores the physics behind planetary motion using Newton's Law of Gravitation and Kepler's Laws of Planetary Motion.

## Key Takeaways

* Gravity governs the motion of planets.
* Orbits are generally elliptical.
* Orbital period increases with distance from the Sun.
* Escape velocity depends on a planet's mass and radius.
* The Solar System can be modeled using surprisingly simple physical laws.
