# 04 - Double Slit Experiment

## Introduction

The Double Slit Experiment is one of the most important experiments in the history of physics.

It demonstrated that matter and light do not behave strictly as particles or strictly as waves.

Instead, quantum objects can exhibit properties of both.
 
The experiment played a major role in the development of modern quantum mechanics.

---

## Historical Background

In the early 1800s, Thomas Young used the double slit experiment to demonstrate that light behaves like a wave.

More than a century later, scientists repeated the experiment using electrons.

To their surprise, electrons produced the same interference pattern as light.

This suggested that particles can also behave like waves.

---

## Experimental Setup

A source emits particles toward a barrier containing two narrow slits.

Behind the barrier is a detection screen.

```text
Particle Source
      |
      V

    |     |
    |     |
    |     |
   Slits

--------------------
    Detection Screen
```

---

## Classical Prediction

If particles behave like tiny balls:

* Some pass through the left slit.
* Some pass through the right slit.

Expected result:

```text
||       ||
```

Two bright regions on the screen.

---

## Quantum Prediction

Quantum particles behave like waves.

Each wave passes through both slits simultaneously.

The waves combine and interfere.

Result:

```text
||||||||||||||||||
```

Alternating bright and dark regions.

This pattern is called an interference pattern.

---

## The Strange Observation

Scientists then attempted to observe which slit each particle passed through.

When measurement devices were installed:

* The interference pattern disappeared.
* The particles behaved like classical particles.

This result remains one of the most fascinating aspects of quantum mechanics.

---

## Wave-Particle Duality

The Double Slit Experiment demonstrates wave-particle duality.

Quantum objects can exhibit:

### Particle Behavior

* Localized impacts
* Discrete detections

### Wave Behavior

* Interference
* Diffraction
* Probability distributions

The observed behavior depends on the experimental setup.

---

## Why Is This Important?

The Double Slit Experiment led to the development of:

* Quantum Mechanics
* Quantum Computing
* Quantum Cryptography
* Quantum Information Theory
* Modern Semiconductor Technology

It fundamentally changed our understanding of reality.

## Key Takeaways

* Quantum particles behave as both waves and particles.
* Interference patterns arise from wave behavior.
* Observation affects experimental outcomes.
* The Double Slit Experiment is one of the foundations of quantum mechanics.
* Reality at the quantum scale behaves very differently from everyday experience.

The Double Slit Experiment remains one of the most profound demonstrations that nature is fundamentally quantum mechanical.
