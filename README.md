# 06 - Exoplanets

## Introduction

Exoplanets are planets that orbit stars outside our Solar System.

The word "exoplanet" means "extrasolar planet."

Since the first confirmed discovery in 1992, astronomers have identified thousands of exoplanets throughout the Milky Way Galaxy.

Some exoplanets are rocky worlds similar to Earth, while others are massive gas giants larger than Jupiter.

The search for exoplanets is one of the most exciting fields in modern astronomy because it helps answer a fundamental question:

**Are we alone in the universe?**

---

# What is an Exoplanet?

An exoplanet is any planet that orbits a star other than the Sun.

Examples:

* Proxima Centauri b
* Kepler-22b
* TRAPPIST-1e

These planets exist far beyond our Solar System.

---

# How Are Exoplanets Discovered?

Directly photographing exoplanets is extremely difficult because stars are much brighter than the planets orbiting them.

Astronomers use indirect methods.

---

## Transit Method

The most successful detection technique.

When a planet passes in front of its star:

* A small amount of starlight is blocked.
* Brightness decreases slightly.
* Telescopes measure this brightness dip.

Repeated dips indicate the presence of a planet.

---

## Radial Velocity Method

A planet's gravity causes its star to wobble slightly.

Astronomers measure changes in the star's motion.

This allows scientists to estimate:

* Planet mass
* Orbital distance

---

# Habitable Zone

The habitable zone is the region around a star where liquid water may exist.

Too close:

* Water evaporates.

Too far:

* Water freezes.

Earth lies within the Sun's habitable zone.

---

# Types of Exoplanets

### Earth-like

Rocky planets similar to Earth.

### Super-Earth

Larger than Earth but smaller than Neptune.

### Mini-Neptune

Intermediate-sized gaseous worlds.

### Gas Giant

Large planets similar to Jupiter and Saturn.

# Famous Exoplanets

### Proxima Centauri b

Closest known exoplanet to Earth.

Distance:

4.24 light-years

---

### Kepler-22b

One of the first potentially habitable exoplanets discovered.

---

### TRAPPIST-1 System

A system containing seven Earth-sized planets.

Several may lie within the habitable zone.

# Key Takeaways

* Exoplanets orbit stars beyond our Solar System.
* Thousands of exoplanets have already been discovered.
* The transit method is the most successful detection technique.
* Habitable zones help identify potentially life-supporting worlds.
* Exoplanet research is helping scientists understand planetary systems throughout the galaxy.

The discovery of exoplanets has transformed our understanding of the universe and continues to expand the search for life beyond Earth.
